Agent Keyboard bundled Chinese pinyin dictionary data
=====================================================

CC-CEDICT
---------
Source: MDBG CC-CEDICT, https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
Referenced work: CEDICT, Copyright (C) 1997, 1998 Paul Andrew Denisowski
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The bundled pinyin dictionary is an adapted database derived from CC-CEDICT.
It contains filtered and normalized simplified entries and pinyin; tone marks
are removed, entries are joined with word-frequency data, and ranking weights
are calculated. This adapted database is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Attribution: CC-CEDICT, published by MDBG; referenced work by Paul Andrew
Denisowski. Changes: filtering, normalization, frequency enrichment, and
ranking-weight calculation.

THUOCL word-document-frequency values are used as an additional ranking
signal. The THUOCL README states that the lexicon is freely available for
research and commercial use. It requests the following acknowledgment and
citation when publishing research results based on THUOCL:

"使用了清华大学开放中文词库"

Han Shiyi, Zhang Yuhui, Ma Yunshan, Tu Cunchao, Guo Zhipeng, Liu Zhiyuan,
Sun Maosong. THUOCL: Tsinghua Open Chinese Lexicon. 2016.

Complete source versions, checksums, filtering rules, and rebuild instructions
are recorded in docs/third-party-data-inventory.md. The third-party data
inventory also records unresolved provenance questions for source corpora.
